## Objective

Develop two deliberately distinct mango formats: a cooked, high-fruit purée preparation that can be squeezed onto the inside of a cold matcha glass and a clarified cordial that can carry mango, sweetness, and acidity into tea, matcha, zero-proof drinks, carbonation, and cocktails without purée-level solids.

## Professional evidence and selected lineage

- PR-0012 is a professional mango-purée cordial benchmark, but its scaling is unresolved and it is not executable as an exact formula.
- PR-0017 directly supports mango purée in an iced matcha-milk build.
- PR-0018 supports a cold-blended, high-purée mango coulis kept in a squeeze bottle for professional service.
- PR-0020 is the selected direct formulation source: 1,000 g Boiron mango purée, 225 g sugar, and 7 g NH pectin, cooked to 103°C, cooled, and whisked smooth.
- PR-0021 is the preferred cordial lineage: Michal Dojčák's enzyme-clarified mango cordial, adjusted to 50 Brix and combined with an acid blend that may be diluted with green tea.
- MONIN Le Fruit Mango is the realistically purchasable commercial squeeze/service control; its proprietary formula is not reconstructed.
- `B-20260831-MANG-V02-01` is a disclosed practical whole-gram scale of PR-0020, classified `ADAPTATION OF SOURCED RECIPE`.

## Purée-preparation track — fixed valid Batch 001 assumptions

- Preferred input: Les Vergers Boiron frozen 100% Alphonso/Kesar mango purée (`SRC-2026-039`/`040`); physical label controls.
- Formula: 286 g Boiron mango purée, 64 g sugar, and 2 g NH pectin. This is derived from PR-0020 at a 2 g pectin scale; the exact 285.714 g purée and 64.286 g sugar results are rounded to whole grams and the 103°C method is retained.
- Texture: NH pectin is an integral PR-0020 ingredient. Any different type or amount is a new sourced adaptation; no original modifier formula is authorised.
- Dairy: acceptable in the drink application; not present in the purée preparation.
- Service: ordinary clear iced-matcha glass and measured fine-nozzle squeeze bottle.
- Handling: no shelf-life claim; actual development-session disposition must be recorded.

## Cordial track — source selected, batch not opened

- Preferred source: `PR-0021` / `SRC-2026-069`.
- Source starting formula: 400 g mango juice with 8 g Töufood Pectinäse and 8 g Töufood Invertäse; seal in a vacuum bag and refrigerate for 24 hours before filtering.
- Source-controlled finish: add sugar to a 1:1 simple-syrup ratio or 50 Brix, then combine equal parts with Citrus Blend already diluted 1:9 in water or a complementary liquid such as green tea.
- Input gate: the source says mango juice. Using the ordered Boiron purée is a declared adaptation and must receive its own batch record.
- Equipment/procurement gate: verify the two enzyme variants, Citrus Blend, German delivery, and a liquid-compatible sealed-bag method. The CASO VC10 must not be assumed suitable for vacuuming the unfrozen liquid.
- Application scope: carbonation and several cocktail families are source-supported; matcha performance remains untested.
- Storage boundary: the source's refrigerated wording is recorded but does not establish a project shelf life.

- Does the 30 ml PR-0017-derived fruit-component dose retain clear mango character with 2 g matcha and 200 ml milk?

- Is the Boiron source sweetness balanced in matcha service?
- Does clarification materially improve clean dispersion and carbonation compared with the purée preparation?
- Does the cordial retain enough ripe mango character after filtration to justify the specialist process?

# Product Brief — PB-003

## Identity and status

- **Product ID:** PB-003
- **Product name:** Mango Matcha Sauce
- **Short code:** MANG

## Objective

Develop a high-fruit mango preparation for an iced matcha drink that can be squeezed onto the inside of a cold clear glass, remain visibly present during construction, and deliver ripe mango after stirring without burying matcha.

## Evidence and formulation boundary

- PR-0012 is a professional mango-purée cordial benchmark, but its scaling is unresolved and it is not executable as an exact formula.
- PR-0017 directly supports mango purée in an iced matcha-milk build.
- PR-0018 supports a cold-blended, high-purée mango coulis kept in a squeeze bottle for professional service.
- MONIN Le Fruit Mango is a commercial service control with about 50% mango plus pectin; it is not the house formula source.
- `B-20260831-MANG-V01-01` is therefore an `ORIGINAL EXPERIMENTAL FORMULATION`.

## Fixed Batch 001 assumptions

- Preferred input: Les Vergers Boiron frozen 100% Alphonso/Kesar mango purée (`SRC-2026-039`/`040`).
- Dairy: permitted; Nestlé Milchmädchen preferred for consistency with PB-001.
- Texture modifier: none. Pectin, xanthan, gellan, starch, or other modifiers require a separate original formulation.
- Service: ordinary clear iced-matcha glass and measured fine-nozzle squeeze bottle.
- Handling: same-day refrigerated development use only; no shelf-life claim.

## Functional and sensory questions

- Does mango's natural body already meet the cling requirement without a modifier?
- Does it squeeze cleanly rather than form a heavy blob or tail?
- Is 40 g sauce enough to retain clear mango character with 2 g matcha and 150 g milk?
- Does the sauce disperse acceptably after three stirs?
- Does condensed milk help structure without making mango dull or excessively sweet?

## Stage decision

Prepare `B-20260831-MANG-V01-01` only with the specified input and verified 0.1 g scale. Run `GC-20260831-PB003-01`; advance to `AT-20260831-003` only under its comparative rule. No production or shelf-life approval is implied.

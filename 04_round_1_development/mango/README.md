## Option 1 — Glass-cling purée preparation

This is a cooked, pectin-set purée preparation—not plain fruit purée and not a cordial. It is a practical whole-gram scale of Les Vergers Boiron's professionally published [mango confit/compote (`PR-0020`)](../../03_professional_recipes/records/PR-0020.md). It is the option intended for squeeze-bottle application inside an iced-matcha glass.

| Ingredient | Batch 001 amount |
|---|---:|
| Les Vergers Boiron 100% mango purée | 286 g |
| Granulated white sugar, first portion | 32 g |
| Granulated white sugar, pectin-premix portion | 32 g |
| NH pectin | 2 g |

Heat the purée and first sugar portion; at 50°C gradually whisk in the sugar–pectin premix. Cook to 103°C, transfer, cool and whisk smooth. See the [full controlled Batch 001 method and provenance](../../05_lab_notebook/batches/B-20260831-MANG-V02-01.md).

## Option 2 — Clarified mango cordial

This is [Michal Dojčák's exact published cordial process (`PR-0021`)](../../03_professional_recipes/records/PR-0021.md), visibly separate from the purée preparation.

| Ingredient | Source amount |
|---|---:|
| Mango juice | 400 g |
| Töufood Pectinäse | 8 g |
| Töufood Invertäse | 8 g |
| Sugar | Add after clarification to reach 50 Brix |
| Töufood Citrus Blend | Dilute 1:9, then use equal volume to the clarified mango syrup |

Seal the mango juice with both enzymes and refrigerate for 24 hours. Filter, add sugar to the source-stated 50 Brix endpoint, dilute the Citrus Blend 1:9 with water or a complementary liquid such as green tea, then combine equal volumes of clarified mango syrup and diluted blend.

The recipe is recorded and visible, but an operational project batch is not yet open. The source uses mango juice—not the ordered Boiron purée—and requires named specialist products plus a liquid-compatible sealed-bag method. Those substitutions cannot be silently invented.

The cordial explicitly supports carbonation, cocktail families and green tea as an acid-blend dilution liquid. The Experimental Cocktail Club mango cordial remains a secondary benchmark with unresolved scaling.

No production formula, shelf life, or safety outcome is approved.

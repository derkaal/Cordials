## Current position

Two formats are intentionally separate. A 100% pistachio paste can provide real nut flavour and body in milk-led drinks; a syrup can provide faster, cleaner dispersion across a broader drinks range. Neither formulation has been invented or selected yet.

The first input candidate is Viani 100% pistachio paste in a 95 g jar (`SRC-2026-074`). METRO Chef peeled pistachio kernels in a 1 kg pack (`SRC-2026-084`) are retained for a later whole-nut cost/flavour comparison, not the first pilot.

MONIN Pistachio 0.7 L (`SRC-2026-073`) is the first commercial syrup control. `PR-0023` records 1883's exact Pistachio & Chaï application as professional evidence; it is not a house syrup recipe.

No production formula, experimental batch, shelf life or safety outcome is approved.

## Required next steps

1. Select a complete professional paste-preparation or syrup formulation before opening a batch.
2. Recheck and record the physical input/control labels, prices and allergen statements.
3. Keep whole METRO kernels deferred until paste-led flavour and likely service volume justify the added process.

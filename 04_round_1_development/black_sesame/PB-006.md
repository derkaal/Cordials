## Objective

Develop a source-traced black-sesame preparation with real sesame for a visually distinctive matcha and milk-drink speciality, comparing purchased 100% paste with a professional seed-to-paste route only when the latter can be measured and executed faithfully.

## Current evidence

- `SRC-2026-082`: 250 g German purchase route for 100% black-sesame paste.
- `PR-0025` / `SRC-2026-081`: Vitamix publishes an exact toasted-seed paste and a black-sesame latte with honey-matcha foam.
- `PR-0026` / `SRC-2026-083`: Health Bar publishes a black-sesame matcha latte and explicitly applies the sesame base to the inner glass wall.
- No sufficiently specific current METRO bulk black-sesame product was verified during this screen.

## Source and process gates

- The Vitamix paste uses 150 g seeds but gives oil and honey in millilitres and salt by teaspoon. Preserve those source units until a documented whole-gram adaptation can be made.
- Using purchased 100% paste in place of the source-made seed/oil/honey paste is an adaptation, not an exact execution.
- Confirm that the available blender can produce a sufficiently fine paste before buying bulk seed.
- Do not open a clear syrup/cordial track unless a complete professional formulation is found.
- Sesame is an allergen; nut-free wording must not imply allergen-free service.

## Functional questions

- Does the sesame base remain visible on the cold glass during construction?
- Does it disperse after stirring without gritty sediment or an oily ring?
- Is the toasted bitterness balanced with matcha and milk?
- Does processing from seed improve flavour or cost enough to justify grinding, cleaning and process variability?

## Stage decision

Proceed with source conversion and input comparison. Use the purchased 100% paste as the low-variable starting point; retain whole toasted seed as a meaningful second route because it is directly supported by `PR-0025`. No batch, shelf-life claim or production approval is open.

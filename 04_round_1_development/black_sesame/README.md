## Current position

Black sesame begins as a paste preparation, not a clear cordial. A German 250 g 100% black-sesame paste (`SRC-2026-082`) provides a convenient input. The exact Vitamix reference (`PR-0025`) starts from toasted seeds, sesame oil and honey, so a whole-seed comparison is potentially useful once measurement and blender-capability gates are resolved.

`PR-0026` records a German commercial matcha recipe that explicitly spreads the black-sesame base on the inside wall of the glass. Japanese commercial references add a paste-based bottom-layer build (`PR-0029`), a powder-based layered matcha (`PR-0030`) and an exact 10 g powder / 150 ml milk manufacturer benchmark (`PR-0031`). These support comparison across formats but do not establish a project formula or performance threshold.

No production formula, experimental batch, shelf life or safety outcome is approved.

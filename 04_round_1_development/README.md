# Round 1 Cordial Development

Round 1 covers Yuzu, Strawberry–Vanilla, Peach–Tea–Thyme, Lychee, Pandan, and Mango. Each subfolder contains a candidate file for verified research, explicit hypotheses, formulation links, evaluation, and decisions.

Candidate scores remain `UNKNOWN` until supported by reliable published evidence or recorded experimental results.

The same established development area also holds separately identified product cases: `pumpkin_matcha/` is the PB-001 sauce case, while `lychee/` PB-002 and `mango/` PB-003 each retain two parallel formats. Their glass-cling purée preparations and filtered/clarified cordials have separate source lineages, service functions, and future tests; neither format replaces the other.

The 2026 autumn/winter extension adds PB-004 Pistachio, PB-005 Roasted Hazelnut, PB-006 Black Sesame and PB-007 Spiced Apple–Pear Cordial. Nut/seed pastes are kept distinct from syrups and fruit purées. PB-007 has a source-traced cordial batch; no other formulation or batch is opened merely because an input or commercial control is purchasable.

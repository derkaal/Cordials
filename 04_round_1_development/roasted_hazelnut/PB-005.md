# Product Brief — PB-005

## Identity and status

- **Product ID:** PB-005
- **Product name:** Roasted Hazelnut Paste Preparation and Syrup
- **Short code:** HAZEL
- **Date opened:** 2026-09-03
- **Status:** ACTIVE — COMMERCIAL SYRUP SELECTED; PASTE APPLICATION AT SOURCE-SELECTION GATE
- **Preparation classification:** Parallel nut-paste preparation and syrup

## Objective

Develop a source-traced purchased roasted-hazelnut paste preparation for milk-led drinks and use a commercial roasted-hazelnut syrup for fast coffee, tea, zero-proof and cocktail service. Do not develop a house syrup at this stage.

## Current evidence

- `SRC-2026-078`: KoRo 500 g paste is 100% freshly roasted hazelnut and the preferred convenient first input.
- `SRC-2026-085`: METRO Chef Römer hazelnuts, 1 kg, are a future whole-nut comparison; the online minimum was four packs at access time and the listing does not say roasted.
- `SRC-2026-077`: MONIN Roasted Hazelnut is the first realistically purchasable German syrup control.
- `SRC-2026-079`: 1883 Roasted Hazelnut provides a second professional syrup benchmark.
- `PR-0024` / `SRC-2026-080`: the exact 1883 Hazelnut Old Fashioned supplies a quantified cocktail use, not a reproducible syrup formula.

## Source and process gates

- No complete professional paste-based beverage formula is selected. A house syrup formula is no longer required because the commercial syrup route was selected on 2026-09-19.
- Do not add sugar, water, dairy, emulsifier or acid to the paste without source lineage.
- A whole-hazelnut route requires sourced roast parameters, skin handling, grinding method and measurable yield before it becomes an executable adaptation.
- A raw-nut price advantage alone is insufficient; compare measured labour, energy, cleaning, loss, smoothness and cost per usable gram.

## Functional questions

- Is the flavour distinctively roasted hazelnut rather than generic sweet café syrup?
- Can the paste disperse without oiling off or masking matcha?
- Does the selected commercial syrup provide sufficient roasted-hazelnut character in coffee and matcha?
- Does whole-nut processing produce a sensory or economic improvement large enough to justify the additional process?

## Stage decision

Use purchased 100% roasted paste for first source-led development and MONIN Roasted Hazelnut as the service syrup. Retain METRO whole hazelnuts as a later comparison only; do not buy the four-pack online minimum. Do not open a house-syrup batch.

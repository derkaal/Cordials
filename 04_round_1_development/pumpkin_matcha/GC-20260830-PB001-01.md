## Question

How do the unmodified pumpkin input and the Boiron-derived glass-cling purée preparation compare with a purchasable commercial café sauce for static wall retention, resistance to a standardised build challenge, and squeeze-bottle service?

No absolute numerical pass threshold is set. The first pilot establishes the observed range and compares samples against the commercial control.

## Exact pilot setup

1. Use the ordinary clear matcha-service glass selected for PB-001. Before testing record maker/model if known, nominal capacity, empty mass, internal height, maximum internal diameter, and whether the side wall is straight or tapered.
2. Use the same cleaned glass model for every valid run. Chill inverted for at least 30 minutes at 4 ± 1 °C; inside wall must be visibly dry.
3. Condition all three samples at 4 ± 1 °C for at least 30 minutes. Record actual sample and glass temperature.
4. Remix each sample exactly as its service instruction requires. For KoRo, use 10 uniform spoon strokes and rest 2 minutes. For P-PUREE-V03, use the source-derived cold spatula smoothing and then rest 2 minutes; record stroke count. For FONTE, follow the physical label; if it says shake, record number/duration of shakes.
5. Run three valid Phase A/B replicates per sample using the 3.0 ± 0.1 g × 60 mm bead, timing, photographs, 90 ± 5 g ice, and 150.0 ± 1.0 g water specified in protocol v0.2.
6. Determine sample order by drawing the three codes before each replicate block. Record the order; do not choose the most attractive sequence.
7. Run five squeeze-dosing attempts for FONTE in its supplied package and five for P-PUREE-V03 in the selected house squeeze bottle. KoRo is tested only if it can pass through the selected bottle without force or modification; otherwise record `INCOMPARABLE — NOT SERVICEABLE UNMODIFIED`.

## Advancement rule

- If P-PUREE-V03 is not worse than C-FONTE on wall descent/build retention and is serviceable from the bottle, retain it for a new source-only matcha application test.
- If P-PUREE-V03 is worse on cling but acceptable in flavour, do not improvise. Research another complete professional/commercial formulation and open a separately traced adaptation.
- If P-PUREE-V03 is better on cling but worse in squeeze service, treat nozzle/process design separately before changing composition.
- If the commercial control itself immediately fails the wall function, retain its data but do not lower PB-001's functional requirement to match it.

## Objective

Develop a two-component pumpkin system containing actual pumpkin for Huong's seasonal iced-matcha special: a high-pumpkin purée preparation for the inside wall of a cold glass and a separate pourable syrup for spice and adjustable sweetness. The wall preparation must remain visibly in place long enough for the drink to be built around it. Whether both components are operationally superior to one combined sauce remains an experimental question.

## Primary drink/application

- Iced pumpkin matcha.
- Exact drink formulation: NOT YET OPENED — a complete professional/commercial source lineage is required under the current source-only rule. `AT-20260830-001` was superseded before execution.
- Intended presentation: visible decorative line, smear, or pattern on the inside wall of a clear cold glass.

## Functional requirements

- Clings to the inside wall of a cold glass.
- Does not rapidly run to the bottom before or during the standard drink build.
- Can be dosed consistently in café service, ideally from a squeeze bottle.
- Disperses acceptably when the customer drinks or stirs; the desired degree of persistence versus integration is TBD.
- Does not create unacceptable separation, clumps, or sediment in the finished drink.
- A sauce, syrup, concentrate, purée-based preparation, or another correct class may be selected; a cordial is not presumed.
- The purée preparation owns decorative glass cling; the syrup owns spice and adjustable sweetness. Do not judge the syrup by the cling requirement.

## Flavour requirements

- Clear pumpkin character.
- Actual pumpkin is mandatory: PUREE-V02 contains 280 g KoRo 100% pumpkin purée, and SYRUP-V02 contains 120 ml KoRo pumpkin purée. Pumpkin spice alone cannot satisfy the product brief.
- Compatible with matcha rather than dominating it.
- Sweetness must contribute materially to the finished drink.
- Spice profile and intensity: TBD; pumpkin character must not be reduced to spice character alone.
- Salt, vanilla, maple, caramelised sugar, dairy, and other supporting flavours: experimental questions, not requirements.

## Visual requirements

- Attractive orange-to-golden pumpkin colour against a clear glass.
- Pattern should remain legible through the service build.
- Target colour coordinates or photographic reference: TBD.
- Colour stability during refrigerated holding: TBD and must be observed.

## Texture requirements

- High enough resistance to gravity at low shear to limit wall run.
- Low enough resistance under squeeze/shear for practical bottle application.

## Success criteria

Before approval, a candidate must:

1. meet a numerical glass-cling threshold established by the controlled benchmark trial;
2. remain visually legible during a repeatable cold-glass build simulation;
3. dispense repeatably from the selected service bottle without clogging or uncontrolled tailing;
4. show clear pumpkin flavour and acceptable matcha balance in a fixed iced-matcha recipe;
5. show no unacceptable separation or gritty texture during the defined service window;
6. have measured yield, dose, preparation time, active labour, cost, and refrigerated observation history;
7. have a conservative storage/discard instruction supported at the appropriate evidence level; and
8. retain full source and batch lineage.

Numerical pass/fail values are `TBD` until benchmark and feasibility data exist; none should be invented in advance.

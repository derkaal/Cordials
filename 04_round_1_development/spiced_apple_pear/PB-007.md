# Product Brief — PB-007

## Identity and status

- **Product ID:** PB-007
- **Product name:** Spiced Apple–Pear Cordial
- **Short code:** APPEAR
- **Date opened:** 2026-09-17
- **Status:** ACTIVE — SOURCE-TRACED BATCH 001 READY TO TEST
- **Preparation classification:** Filtered fruit-juice cordial

## Objective

Develop one clear winter cordial that can be evaluated in both matcha and coffee without the solids and bottle-flow constraints of a fruit purée. Apple supplies familiar baked-fruit acidity; pear supplies softer aroma and sweetness; allspice and cinnamon provide winter character.

## Provenance decision

- PR-0035 provides the complete cordial structure: juice, sugar, allspice, cinnamon, citric acid and dissolve/cool/strain method.
- PR-0036 provides a professional equal-volume apple–pear pairing.
- Batch 001 changes PR-0035's 500 ml pear juice to 250 ml apple juice plus 250 ml pear juice. No other source ingredient or quantity is changed.
- Because this replacement is deliberate, Batch 001 is an `ADAPTATION OF SOURCED RECIPE`.

## Functional requirements

- Strains cleanly and doses consistently.
- Retains recognisable apple and pear beside milk, matcha or espresso.
- Spice reads as winter warmth rather than gritty powder or dominant allspice.
- Acidity must not cause unacceptable milk splitting; this is an application-test question, not assumed performance.
- Hot and iced suitability must be evaluated separately.

## Commercial control

MONIN Apple Pie 0.7 L (`SRC-2026-097`) is realistically purchasable in Germany and is relevant to apple/cinnamon café service. It does not contain a declared pear identity, so the first comparison must score pear recognition separately and must not call MONIN an exact like-for-like control.

## Application boundary

No complete sourced matcha or coffee build has yet been selected. Do not invent a finished drink recipe. First measure the cordial and control, then select or adapt a professionally published application for each beverage.

## Shelf-life boundary

No shelf life is established for the adapted cordial. Brix is a process and comparison measurement, not a safety validation.

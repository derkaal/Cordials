The sauce direction is a practical whole-gram scale and canned-input adaptation of Les Vergers Boiron's professionally published lychee confit/compote (`PR-0019`). It remains the glass-cling option.

The separate cordial direction uses Barney Toy's filtered canned-lychee cordial (`PR-0022`) as its preferred professional lineage. It remains distinct from the sauce and is intended for broader dilution across tea, matcha, zero-proof drinks, and cocktails. A cordial batch will be opened only after the user's can size and contents are recorded.

No production formula, shelf life, or safety outcome is approved.

## Option 1 — Glass-cling purée preparation

This is a cooked, pectin-set purée preparation—not plain fruit purée and not a cordial. It is a practical whole-gram scale and canned-input adaptation of Les Vergers Boiron's professionally published [lychee confit/compote (`PR-0019`)](../../03_professional_recipes/records/PR-0019.md). It is the option intended for squeeze-bottle application inside an iced-matcha glass.

| Ingredient | Batch 001 amount |
|---|---:|
| Smooth purée made from drained canned lychees | 220 g |
| Granulated white sugar, first portion | 25 g |
| Granulated white sugar, pectin-premix portion | 25 g |
| NH pectin | 2 g |

Heat the purée and first sugar portion; at 50°C gradually whisk in the sugar–pectin premix. Cook to 103°C, transfer, cool and whisk smooth. At 220 g purée, the exact PR-0019 scale would be 49.5 g sugar and 1.98 g pectin; the 50 g / 2 g working amounts are disclosed café-practical adaptations. See the [full controlled Batch 001 method and provenance](../../05_lab_notebook/batches/B-20260905-LYCH-V03-01.md).

## Option 2 — Filtered lychee cordial

This is [Barney Toy's exact published cordial formula (`PR-0022`)](../../03_professional_recipes/records/PR-0022.md), visibly separate from the purée preparation.

| Ingredient | Source amount |
|---|---:|
| Canned lychees | 1 × 560 g can |
| Caster sugar | 150 g |
| Citric acid | 10 g |
| Sea salt | 1 g |

Blend all ingredients on high for 1 minute and then on medium for 5 minutes. Filter under refrigeration through a coffee filter/Chemex or cheesecloth/strainer, preferably overnight. The source states a 600 ml yield.

The recipe is recorded and visible, but an operational project batch is not yet open. Before scaling it to the can on hand, record the can's total size, drained fruit, retained liquid, syrup type and Brix. The source does not separately state the fruit/liquid split, so that uncertainty must not be silently filled in.

The cordial is intended for broader dilution across tea, matcha, zero-proof drinks and cocktails. Cocktail/highball use is sourced; the other applications remain to be tested.

No production formula, shelf life, or safety outcome is approved.

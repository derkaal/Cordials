# Product Brief — PB-002

## Identity and status

- **Product ID:** PB-002
- **Product name:** Lychee Matcha Sauce
- **Short code:** LYCH
- **Date opened:** 2026-08-31
- **Status:** ACTIVE — PURÉE-BASED BATCH 001 / COMPARATIVE PILOT PLANNED
- **Preparation classification:** Sauce; original experimental formulation

## Objective

Develop a high-fruit lychee preparation for an iced matcha drink that can be squeezed onto the inside of a cold clear glass, remain visibly present during construction, and deliver recognisable floral lychee after stirring without overwhelming matcha.

## Evidence and formulation boundary

- PR-0007 provides a professional purée + salt + citric-acid lychee flavour precedent, but contains lychee liqueur.
- PR-0016 provides a direct café lychee–matcha–milk precedent, but uses fresh juice and coconut milk rather than a wall-cling sauce.
- MONIN Le Fruit Lychee is a commercial service control with approximately 50% lychee equivalent plus pectin/citrus fibre; it is not the house formula source.
- `B-20260831-LYCH-V01-01` is therefore an `ORIGINAL EXPERIMENTAL FORMULATION`, not an adaptation of either professional recipe.

## Fixed Batch 001 assumptions

- Preferred input: Ponthier unsweetened lychee purée, retailer-stated 99.9% lychee plus ascorbic acid (`SRC-2026-038`); physical label controls.
- Dairy: permitted; Nestlé Milchmädchen preferred for consistency with PB-001.
- Texture modifier: none. Pectin, xanthan, gellan, starch, or other modifiers require a separate original formulation.
- Service: ordinary clear iced-matcha glass and measured fine-nozzle squeeze bottle.
- Handling: same-day refrigerated development use only; no shelf-life claim.

## Functional and sensory questions

- Does the pale sauce remain visible on glass and against milk?
- Does it cling and squeeze at least as well as the MONIN control?
- Is 40 g sauce enough to retain clear lychee character with 2 g matcha and 150 g milk?
- Does citric acid or fruit acidity create graininess when condensed milk is added?
- Does the floral character survive the matcha build and three-stir integration?

## Stage decision

Prepare `B-20260831-LYCH-V01-01` only with the specified input and verified 0.1 g scale. Run `GC-20260831-PB002-01`; advance to `AT-20260831-002` only under its comparative rule. No production or shelf-life approval is implied.

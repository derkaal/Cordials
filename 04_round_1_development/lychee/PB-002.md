# Product Brief — PB-002

## Identity and status

- **Product ID:** PB-002
- **Product name:** Lychee Sauce and Cordial
- **Short code:** LYCH
- **Date opened:** 2026-08-31
- **Status:** ACTIVE — DUAL-FORMAT DEVELOPMENT; SAUCE BATCH PLANNED; CORDIAL SOURCE SELECTED
- **Preparation classification:** Parallel glass-cling sauce and filtered cordial

## Objective

Develop two deliberately distinct lychee formats: a high-fruit sauce that can be squeezed onto the inside of a cold matcha glass and a filtered cordial that can carry lychee, sweetness, and acidity into tea, matcha, zero-proof drinks, and cocktails without purée-level solids.

## Professional evidence and selected lineage

- PR-0019 is the selected direct formulation source: 1,000 g Boiron lychee purée, 225 g sugar, and 9 g NH pectin, cooked to 103°C, cooled, and whisked smooth.
- PR-0016 provides a direct café lychee–matcha–milk precedent, but not the sauce structure.
- PR-0007 remains an alcoholic flavour precedent only.
- PR-0022 is the preferred first cordial lineage: Barney Toy's zero-proof salted lychee cordial made from a 560 g can, sugar, citric acid, and sea salt, then filtered cold. Its 35 ml highball use is sourced.
- MONIN Le Fruit Lychee is the realistically purchasable commercial squeeze/service control; its proprietary formula is not reconstructed.
- `B-20260831-LYCH-V02-01` is a disclosed practical whole-gram scale and canned-fruit input substitution from PR-0019, classified `ADAPTATION OF SOURCED RECIPE`.

- Handling: no shelf-life claim; actual development-session disposition must be recorded.

## Cordial track — source selected, batch not opened

- Preferred source: `PR-0022` / `SRC-2026-071`.
- Source formula: one 560 g can of lychees, 150 g caster sugar, 10 g citric acid, and 1 g sea salt; blend for six minutes total and filter cold, preferably overnight.
- Source yield: 600 ml. This is not a project-measured yield.
- Input gate: record the user's can total size, drained fruit, retained liquid, syrup type, and Brix before deciding whether the source can be executed exactly.
- Adaptation rule: a differently sized can requires a separately written, whole-gram scaled adaptation. Do not improvise at the bench.
- Application scope: cocktail/highball use is sourced; tea, matcha, and zero-proof performance remain untested.
- Storage boundary: the source's refrigerated wording is recorded but does not establish a project shelf life.

## Functional and sensory questions

- Does the pale sauce remain visible on glass and against milk?
- How does it compare with MONIN for cling, squeeze control, and legibility?
- Does the 30 ml PR-0017-derived fruit-component dose retain clear lychee character with 2 g matcha and 200 ml milk?
- Does the cooked pectin structure disperse acceptably after stirring?
- Does the floral character survive the matcha build and three-stir integration?
- Does the filtered cordial disperse more cleanly than the sauce in tea, matcha, and carbonated service?
- Does removing purée solids preserve enough recognisable lychee aroma to justify the cordial format?

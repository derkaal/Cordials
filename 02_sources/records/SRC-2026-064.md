## Extracted claims

- *Clostridium botulinum* can form a potent neurotoxin under oxygen-free conditions.
- BfR specifically identifies vacuum packaging as an oxygen-free condition and stresses maintenance of the cold chain to prevent growth.
- Relevant contamination may not cause a detectable smell, gas formation, or visible deterioration.

## Relevance and limitation

Vacuum sealing must not be treated as a stand-alone safety or shelf-life control. This source supports a conservative, HACCP-based boundary; it does not set a validated life for the fruit sauces.

# Source Record — SRC-2026-051

## Bibliographic identity

- **Author/creator:** European Food Safety Authority
- **Title:** Re-evaluation of erythritol (E 968) as a food additive
- **Source type:** EU scientific risk assessment / plain-language summary
- **URL:** https://www.efsa.europa.eu/en/plain-language-summary/re-evaluation-erythritol-e-968-food-additive
- **Accessed date:** 2026-08-31
- **Status:** VALIDATED SAFETY ASSESSMENT

## Extracted claims

- EFSA established an ADI of 0.5 g erythritol/kg body weight/day, primarily to protect against laxative effects and associated electrolyte disturbance.
- Available evidence did not establish a causal relationship between dietary erythritol and cardiovascular disease; further research was recommended.
- High consumers may exceed the ADI.

## Relevance and limitation

Controls both overstatement of cardiovascular harm and an unsupported health halo. It does not show sensory suitability in dairy/pumpkin/fruit sauces.

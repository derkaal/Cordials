# Source Record — SRC-2026-060

## Bibliographic identity

- **Author/creator:** EFSA Panel on Dietetic Products, Nutrition and Allergies
- **Title:** Scientific Opinion on the energy conversion factor of D-tagatose for labelling purposes
- **Source type:** Peer-reviewed EU scientific opinion
- **URL:** https://efsa.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.2903/j.efsa.2016.4630
- **Accessed date:** 2026-08-31
- **Status:** VALIDATED SCIENTIFIC/REGULATORY EVIDENCE

## Extracted claims

- EFSA concluded that a rounded estimate of 3 kcal/g (12.5 kJ/g) was appropriate based on available metabolisable-energy data.
- EFSA stated that available data were insufficient to derive a more accurate metabolisable-energy value.
- The opinion records EU novel-food authorisation of D-tagatose in June 2005.

## Relevance and limitation

Supports a lower energy value than sucrose but also the uncertainty boundary. It does not establish a health benefit, sensory equivalence, gastrointestinal tolerance for a café dose, or menu-claim eligibility.

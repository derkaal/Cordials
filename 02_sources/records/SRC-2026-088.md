## Extracted claims

The one-cup recipe uses one tablespoon black-sesame powder, one teaspoon syrup or honey to taste, 100–150 ml milk, 1.5 teaspoons/4 g matcha and 50–60 ml hot water. The sesame powder and sweetener are smoothed with about one tablespoon of warmed milk before the remaining milk and whisked matcha are layered.

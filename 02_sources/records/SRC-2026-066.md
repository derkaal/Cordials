## Extracted claims

- For liquid-based foods, the manual instructs the user to freeze the food in a suitable dish first, then vacuum seal, label, and return it to the freezer once solid.
- The manual requires the bag mouth to be clean, dry, and free of wrinkles for sealing.

## Relevance and limitation

The VC10 is an external-suction sealer, so a flowable sauce should not be drawn directly into a bag. The source supports freeze-first packaging, not a shelf-life claim or a reason to sous-vide-cook the sauce.

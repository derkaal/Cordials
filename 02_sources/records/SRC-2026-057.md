# Source Record — SRC-2026-057

## Bibliographic identity

- **Author/creator:** Witkowski et al.
- **Title:** Xylitol is prothrombotic and associated with cardiovascular risk
- **Source type:** Peer-reviewed human observational, mechanistic, animal, and small intervention study
- **URL:** https://academic.oup.com/eurheartj/article/45/27/2439/7683453
- **Accessed date:** 2026-08-31
- **Status:** VALIDATED PRIMARY SCIENTIFIC SOURCE

## Extracted claims

- Higher fasting plasma xylitol was associated with incident major adverse cardiovascular events in the validation cohort.
- Mechanistic work found increased platelet responsiveness and thrombosis potential; a ten-person ingestion study found increased platelet responsiveness after 30 g xylitol.
- The authors state that further cardiovascular-safety studies are warranted.

## Relevance and limitation

Creates a serious caution against marketing xylitol as straightforwardly healthier. Observational associations can be confounded, the intervention was small and acute, and the study does not establish long-term population-level causality.

# Source Record — SRC-2026-048

## Bibliographic identity

- **Author/creator:** World Health Organization
- **Title:** Use of non-sugar sweeteners: WHO guideline
- **Source type:** International public-health guideline
- **URL:** https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240073616
- **Accessed date:** 2026-08-31
- **Status:** VALIDATED HEALTH GUIDANCE

## Extracted claims

- WHO conditionally recommends against using non-sugar sweeteners for weight control or reducing non-communicable-disease risk.
- The recommendation is based on the absence of long-term benefit for reducing body fat and possible undesirable long-term associations; confounding remains a limitation.
- The recommendation does not apply to sugar alcohols/polyols.

## Relevance and limitation

Prevents treating stevia, sucralose, and other non-sugar sweeteners as automatically healthier. It is population guidance, not a formulation, toxicological limit, or claim that every use is harmful.

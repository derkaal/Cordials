# Source Record — SRC-2026-058

## Bibliographic identity

- **Author/creator:** World Health Organization
- **Title:** Guideline: sugars intake for adults and children
- **Source type:** International public-health guideline
- **URL:** https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789241549028
- **Accessed date:** 2026-08-31
- **Status:** VALIDATED HEALTH GUIDANCE

## Extracted claims

- WHO recommends reducing free-sugar intake to less than 10% of total energy and conditionally suggests below 5%.
- Free sugars include sugars added by the manufacturer/cook/consumer and sugars naturally present in honey, syrups, fruit juices, and fruit-juice concentrates.

## Relevance and limitation

Rejects the assumption that honey, agave, maple, date syrup, or fruit-juice concentrate is automatically a health improvement over sucrose. It is population guidance, not a per-drink approval threshold.

## Extracted formulation

The source combines equal volumes—four cups each—of apple cider and pear juice or nectar with dark brown sugar, cinnamon sticks, cloves, black peppercorns and allspice berries. It simmers for five minutes, steeps off heat for at least twenty minutes, and is strained before service.

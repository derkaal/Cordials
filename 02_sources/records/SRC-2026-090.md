## Extracted claims

The two-cup recipe uses three cups milk, two tablespoons pumpkin purée or more to preference, two tablespoons sugar, 1.5 teaspoons pumpkin-pie spice, two tablespoons matcha, four tablespoons water and optional whipped cream. It also publishes an alternate spice breakdown of one teaspoon cinnamon plus one-quarter teaspoon each ginger and nutmeg. The milk, pumpkin and sugar are warmed and whisked smooth before the spice and prepared matcha are incorporated.

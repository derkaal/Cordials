## Extracted claims

- Sous vide mode cooks sealed pouches in an accurately regulated water bath.
- The manual requires a bag suitable for food use, removal of as much air as possible, full submersion, and the supplied temperature probe in the water.
- The default sous-vide setting is 60°C; the timer starts once the water reaches the set temperature.
- The manual's listed sous-vide applications run from 55°C for fish to 80°C for root vegetables.

## Relevance and limitation

The unit is useful for repeatable low-temperature bag cooking. It is not the right tool for `PR-0019` or `PR-0020`, which require the fruit preparation itself to reach 103°C in an open vessel. The manual does not validate a shelf life for any project sauce.

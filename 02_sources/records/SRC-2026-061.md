## Extracted claims

- The chart gives a common base of 1,000 g Les Vergers Boiron fruit purée.
- For lychee it specifies 225 g sugar and 9 g NH pectin.
- For mango it specifies 225 g sugar and 7 g NH pectin.
- The method heats the purée with half the sugar to 50°C, adds the remaining sugar pre-mixed with NH pectin, cooks to 103°C, covers and cools, then whisks the cold preparation smooth.

## Relevance and limitation

This is the strongest complete professional source found for a high-fruit lychee or mango preparation whose structure is deliberately built with fruit-specific NH-pectin quantities. It supports `PR-0019` and `PR-0020`. It does not state squeeze-bottle performance, glass cling, matcha compatibility, yield, storage, or shelf life.

## Extracted claims

- BfR reported 19 authorised sweetening additives in the EU at the 2025 update.
- Eleven are commonly described as high-intensity sweeteners and eight as sugar substitutes/polyols, including xylitol (E 967) and erythritol (E 968).
- EU authorisation requires a safety assessment and ingredient-list declaration.

## Relevance and limitation

Establishes current German/EU authorisation context. Authorisation is not evidence that a sweetener improves long-term health or reproduces sucrose functionality.

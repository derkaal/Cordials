## Extracted claims

The manufacturer base preparation is 10 g Kuki Kurogoma Latte Powder with 150 ml milk or soy milk, stirred thoroughly. The same sheet gives quantified café and alcoholic applications, including a frappe and amaretto-milk serve. These examples are useful evidence that the commercial powder is designed for cross-category service.

## Limitations

- Kuki Kurogoma Latte Powder is a proprietary mixture of black-sesame powder, roasted soybean flour and salt. It is not interchangeable with 100% sesame paste.
- The source states a packaged-product shelf life. That statement does not establish shelf life for a prepared drink or any project batch.
- Only the simplest manufacturer base is indexed as PR-0031; other applications remain in this source record unless separately needed.

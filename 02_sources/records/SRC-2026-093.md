## Source-stated formulation and process

The published recipe uses two small Hokkaido pumpkins (approximately 1,400 g total), 30 g butter, salt, pepper and optional nutmeg. It specifies a 200°C conventional oven, large pumpkin pieces, and 40–50 minutes on a parchment-lined tray until completely tender, followed by blending until smooth.

The source's professional tips specifically identify Hokkaido/Red Kuri as suitable because its dense, relatively low-moisture flesh gives a thick, creamy purée. They also permit roasting it with the skin on after washing and removing the seeds and stringy pulp, then blending skin and flesh. A fine sieve is suggested for an extra-smooth result.

## Project interpretation

- The roasting, skin-on option, blending and sieving are usable professional process evidence for preparing the user's Red Kuri squash.
- Butter, salt, pepper, nutmeg and any thinning liquid are omitted from the PB-001 input preparation. This is a disclosed adaptation, necessary to keep the input a neutral 100% pumpkin purée comparable with the KoRo and Boiron bases.
- The source's time is retained as a guide, while complete tenderness is the controlling endpoint because squash size and piece geometry vary.
- No source shelf-life statement is transferred to PB-001.

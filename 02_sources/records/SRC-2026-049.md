## Extracted claims

- Sweeteners may reduce sugar and energy and generally have little effect on blood glucose, but evidence on weight, metabolism, and the microbiome is heterogeneous.
- DGE states that reducing the preference for sweetness should receive more emphasis than merely replacing sugar with sweeteners.
- High-intensity sweeteners contribute virtually no volume and can create bitter/metallic off-notes at higher concentrations; manufacturers often blend them.
- DGE lists relative sweetness of xylitol at 1 and erythritol at 0.7 versus sucrose.

## Relevance and limitation

Current German public-health and sensory overview. It does not validate any Cordials formula or establish a health benefit for a specific product.

## Extracted claims

Modernist Pantry states that its teaspoon figures are averages from three weight measurements per ingredient. Its table reports NH pectin at 3.6 g per teaspoon. The page also warns that volume-to-weight results vary by ingredient and by how the spoon is filled.

## Project interpretation

- At this reference density, one level 1/2 teaspoon of NH pectin is approximately 1.8 g.
- This is a practical volume measure, not an exact conversion for the Sosa product in the café.
- The first execution should use one standard level 5 ml measuring spoon and may record the actual mass as an observation if a scale is available.
- Changing the planned 2 g dose to 1/2 teaspoon is a disclosed adaptation of the source-derived batch, not an exact execution of the professional gram formula.

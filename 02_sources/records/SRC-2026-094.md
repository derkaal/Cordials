## Extracted formulation and process

The source uses 750 g pumpkin or butternut squash. For steaming, it directs the operator to peel and seed the pumpkin, cut it into evenly sized cubes, steam the cubes over simmering water for 10 minutes, test with a knife, and cook for a further 5 minutes if not cooked through. It then directs mashing and cooling.

## Project interpretation

- This provides a lower-scorch-risk, water-free-contact cooking method for the replacement Red Kuri input trial.
- The source's 750 g quantity is retained in the exact recipe record, but the project batch measures the user's actual prepared mass because the squash size is not known.
- Blending instead of mashing is a disclosed adaptation required by the squeeze-bottle application.
- No shelf-life claim is transferred.

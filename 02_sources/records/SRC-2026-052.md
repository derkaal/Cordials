- **Accessed date:** 2026-08-31

## Extracted claims

- The permitted claim framework includes lower post-prandial blood-glucose rise when sugars are replaced by specified sugar replacers, including D-tagatose and isomaltulose, and the applicable use conditions are met.
- The claim is conditional on qualifying replacement/reduction; it is not a general declaration that a product is healthy.

## Relevance and limitation

Supports the narrow regulatory basis for considering D-tagatose or isomaltulose. It does not approve a claim for an uncalculated café recipe.

## Extracted claims

The one-serving recipe uses 20 g unsweetened black-sesame paste, approximately one tablespoon liquid sugar, 15 g J Taste Multipurpose Matcha Powder, 150 ml cold milk and ice as needed. Fifty millilitres of the milk are used to disperse the matcha powder. The sesame base is placed at the bottom of the glass before ice, matcha and remaining milk.

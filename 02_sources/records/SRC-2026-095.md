## Extracted formulation

The published spiced pear cordial combines 500 ml pear juice, 250 ml granulated sugar, 15 ml cracked whole allspice, one cinnamon stick and 5 ml citric acid. It is simmered only until the sugar dissolves, cooled, strained and bottled.

## Project interpretation

- This is the direct structural source for PB-007 Batch 001.
- Replacing half the pear juice with apple juice is a disclosed adaptation supported by the equal-volume apple–pear pairing in SRC-2026-096.
- Converting the source's 250 ml sugar to a convenient gram amount is not authorised without a recorded conversion or measured source-volume mass. Batch 001 therefore retains 250 ml sugar and records its actual mass.
- The source's storage statement is not transferred to the adapted product.

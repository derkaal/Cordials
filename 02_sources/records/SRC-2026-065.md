## Extracted claims

- Fruit and vegetables should be packed with as little trapped air as practical in freezer-suitable packaging to reduce freezer burn and quality loss.
- Known-use quantities should be measured and frozen as portions.
- Packages should be labelled with contents, quantity, and date.
- Fruit can be thawed gently in the refrigerator.

## Relevance and limitation

Supports portioning and frozen quality handling after a sauce has passed flavour, texture, and service tests. It does not validate microbial shelf life, freeze–thaw quality for these pectin sauces, or repeated thaw/refreeze cycles.

## Authority and relevance

- **Professional/scientific context:** Uniform USDA method for Bostwick consistency of tomato paste/purée.
- **Relevance:** Supports controlling sample concentration where applicable, temperature, remixing, elapsed time, distance, repeats, and agreement in a low-cost consistency comparison.
- **Limitations:** Tomato-specific horizontal-flow method at 20 ± 1 °C. It does not validate the PB-001 vertical cold-glass method or provide a transferable pumpkin-sauce pass threshold.

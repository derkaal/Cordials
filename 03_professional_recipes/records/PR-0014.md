## Source-stated method

1. Combine water and sugar in a 2-qt. (1.9-L) saucepan and bring to a boil.
2. Add sweetened condensed milk, pumpkin puree, pumpkin pie spice, and salt; whisk and cook for 1 minute.

The remaining source directions prepare espresso and frothed milk, add syrup to coffee, finish with milk, and top with spice.

## Source-stated method

1. Heat the purée with half the sugar to 50°C.
2. Mix the remaining sugar with the NH pectin and add it to the purée.
3. Continue cooking until the preparation reaches 103°C.
4. Pour onto a tray, cover with film, and leave to cool.
5. Once cold, mix with a spatula until smooth, then pipe as a filling.

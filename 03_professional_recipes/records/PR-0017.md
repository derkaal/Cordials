## Source-stated formulation

- 2 g matcha
- 60 ml warm water around 80°C
- 200 ml cold plant milk
- 2 tbsp mango purée
- ice; quantity not stated
- purée made from one fresh mango or one cup frozen mango chunks, with 2 tbsp maple syrup optional

## Source-stated method

Whisk matcha with warm water. Place mango purée at the bottom of the glass, add ice and milk, then slowly pour matcha over the ice.

## Source-stated method

1. Mix the liquid sugar thoroughly into the black-sesame paste.
2. Reserve 50 ml of the milk for the matcha layer. First work about one tablespoon of that milk into the named matcha powder, then mix in the rest of the 50 ml.
3. Put the sesame mixture in the bottom of the glass and add ice.
4. Pour in the prepared matcha mixture, then the remaining milk.

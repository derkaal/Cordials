## Source-stated method

1. Weigh the mango juice and both enzymes, seal them in a vacuum bag, and refrigerate for 24 hours.
2. Strain through a superbag or layers of cheesecloth. The source expects about 350 ml clarified yield.
3. Measure the clarified liquid and add sugar until the mix reaches a 1:1 simple-syrup ratio or 50 Brix.
4. Dilute Citrus Blend 1:9 with water or another complementary liquid such as green tea.
5. Combine equal parts clarified mango syrup and diluted Citrus Blend, then bottle.

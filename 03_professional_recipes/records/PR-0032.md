## Source-stated method

1. Mix the matcha with the water until smooth and free of lumps.
2. Warm the milk, pumpkin purée and sugar over low heat while whisking until smooth.
3. Remove from the heat; add the spice and prepared matcha and whisk smooth.
4. Divide between two cups and add optional whipped cream and spice.

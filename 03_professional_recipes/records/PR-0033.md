## Source-stated method

1. Heat a conventional oven to 200°C.
2. Rinse, peel and halve the pumpkins, then cut them into large pieces.
3. Arrange on a parchment-lined tray and bake for 40–50 minutes, until completely tender.
4. Process the pumpkin with the butter, salt, pepper and optional nutmeg until smooth.

The source's tips additionally permit Hokkaido/Red Kuri to be washed, deseeded, roasted and blended with its edible skin. They suggest passing the purée through a fine sieve when an extra-smooth result is wanted.

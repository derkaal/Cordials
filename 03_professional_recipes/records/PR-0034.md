## Source-stated steaming method

1. Peel and seed the pumpkin, then cut it into evenly sized cubes.
2. Put the cubes in a steamer or colander over simmering water and cook for 10 minutes.
3. Test with the point of a knife; cook for a further 5 minutes if not cooked through.
4. Mash and leave to cool.

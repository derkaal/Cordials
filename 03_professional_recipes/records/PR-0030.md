## Source-stated method

1. Warm the milk to approximately 60–70°C.
2. In a glass, combine the sesame powder and chosen sweetener. Add about one tablespoon of the warm milk and stir smooth.
3. Add the remaining milk.
4. Whisk the matcha with the hot water until smooth and frothy.
5. Gently layer the matcha over the milk.

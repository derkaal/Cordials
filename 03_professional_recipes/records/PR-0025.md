## Source-stated method

1. Put the paste ingredients into the Vitamix container in the stated order.
2. Run the Dips & Spreads Program, or start low, increase quickly to high and blend for 60 seconds.
3. Reserve 50 g paste for the latte and refrigerate the remainder in a covered container.
4. Blend 720 ml soy milk with 50 g paste using the Hot Soup Program, or start low, increase quickly to high and blend for 7 minutes 30 seconds.
5. Blend the cold-foam ingredients from low to high for 15 seconds in the Aer Disc container.
6. Pour the sesame latte into glasses with space at the rim, add the foam and optionally garnish with black sesame seeds. For iced service, chill or pour over ice.

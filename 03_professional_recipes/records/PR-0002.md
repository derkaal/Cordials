## Source formulation

- 4 lb ripe strawberries
- 1 cup granulated sugar
- 1 cup water
- zest of 1/2 lemon
- 1 vanilla bean, split and scraped
- Combine in a saucepan; bring to a boil while stirring, reduce to low, simmer about 25 minutes, cool, and strain.

The exact recipe is licensed/reprinted by Epicurious from *Speakeasy* by Jason Kosmas and Dushan Zaric. Preserve the source description that the result is between a purée and syrup.

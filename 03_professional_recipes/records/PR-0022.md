## Source-stated method

1. Add all ingredients to a blender.
2. Blend on high for 1 minute, then on medium for 5 minutes.
3. Filter into a large jug in the refrigerator using a coffee filter and Chemex, or cheesecloth and a strainer; the source says this is best done overnight.
4. Bottle for use.

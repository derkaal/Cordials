## Source-stated formulation and method

- Ceremonial Hikari matcha; exact mass not stated
- 2 oz hot water
- 3 oz fresh lychee juice, produced by blending 10–15 peeled and pitted lychees for 30 seconds
- 6 oz coconut milk
- 0.25 ml gynostemma
- 1 tsp maple syrup
- ice

Whisk matcha with hot water. Shake lychee juice, coconut milk, gynostemma, maple syrup, and ice; pour into a tall glass and top with matcha.

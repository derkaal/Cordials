# Food Science, Preservation, and Shelf-Life Evidence Review

Status: **INITIAL VACUUM/FREEZING BOUNDARY VALIDATED; WIDER RESEARCH IMPORT OPEN**

This review will link each preservation, pH, Brix, storage, spoilage, and safety claim to credible scientific or professional source records. No shelf-life or safety conclusion is currently established.

## Current equipment boundary

- BfR states that *C. botulinum* can form toxin under oxygen-free conditions and specifically identifies vacuum packaging as a condition requiring an intact cold chain (`SRC-2026-064`). Vacuum is therefore packaging, not shelf-life validation.
- BZfE supports portioning, freezer-suitable low-air packaging, labelling with contents/quantity/date, and refrigerator thawing for fruit (`SRC-2026-065`).
- CASO instructs users to freeze flowable foods solid before using the VC10 external vacuum sealer (`SRC-2026-066`).
- No storage duration is assigned to PB-001, PB-002, or PB-003. Freeze–thaw quality testing begins only after flavour, texture, glass cling, squeeze service, and drink performance pass.

# Equipment and Procurement Research

Status: **PB-001 EQUIPMENT RECORDED; WIDER RESEARCH IMPORT STILL OPEN**

## PB-001 equipment position — 2026-08-30

- Russell Hobbs 25630-56 is user-confirmed available. It can support future controlled-temperature or larger-batch work, but is not preferred for the approximately 362 g V02 saucepan batch.
- Proster 0–80% Brix hand refractometer is ordered. Receipt, manual inspection, calibration, and repeatability checks are required before its results enter the lab record.
- CASO VC 10 and two Bonsenkitchen 28 × 600 cm vacuum rolls are ordered. They are compatible by stated width, but are not required for PB-001 V02.
- Vacuum sealing or sous-vide equipment does not create or validate a shelf-life claim. Any preservation experiment requires a separate formulation/process record and appropriate safety evidence.

## PB-002/PB-003 equipment decision — 2026-08-31

- PR-0019 and PR-0020 require the sauce itself to reach 103°C. Use a small saucepan and thermometer; the Russell Hobbs water-bath mode is not required for these batches.
- The cooker remains useful when a later professional source actually specifies controlled low-temperature sealed-pouch processing. It can improve temperature repeatability, but it does not independently make a product last longer.
- Vacuum sealing is most useful here for compact, low-air frozen portions after the sauce has passed flavour, cling, squeeze, and drink tests. BfR warns that vacuum creates an oxygen-free environment, so refrigerated vacuum storage requires appropriate controls and is not an automatic extension.
- The VC10 manual directs users to freeze liquid foods first, then vacuum seal and return them to the freezer. Use single-service portions, label batch/quantity/freeze date, thaw under the café procedure, and evaluate freeze–thaw quality separately.

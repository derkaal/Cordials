## Why this is an adaptation

PR-0014 is an `EXACT SOURCED RECIPE`, stated primarily in cups/teaspoons. V02 is a practical approximately half-scale, weight-based bench adaptation. It keeps the same ingredient set and source cooking sequence, but uses deliberately rounded gram quantities that can be weighed on a 0.1 g scale. These are operational targets, not claimed exact conversions of the source volumes.

V01's exact 25% linear scale was superseded before execution because its 31.25 ml and sub-millilitre quantities were needlessly difficult to measure. V02 is the smallest useful scale at which both spice and salt can be weighed directly with the specified scale.

## Planned method

1. Photograph and record the physical labels, net contents, best-before dates, and lots for pumpkin purée, condensed milk, and spice mix.
2. Confirm the scale reads to 0.1 g and passes the available check. Clean and sanitise utensils, saucepan, receiving vessel, and house squeeze bottle using the café's established procedure; record the procedure actually used.
3. Place the saucepan on the scale and tare it. Weigh 60 g water, then add 50 g sugar. Record actual amounts if either misses its target.
4. Heat while stirring until the source condition “bring to a boil” is reached; record elapsed time and observed temperature without inventing a target.
5. Add 190 g condensed milk, 60 g pumpkin purée, 1.0 g pumpkin spice, and 0.5 g salt. Weigh each separately in a tared vessel before adding so cumulative scale readings cannot hide an error.
6. Whisk until visibly uniform and cook for exactly 1 minute, matching PR-0014; record temperature at start/end and any scorching, lumps, or separation.
7. Remove from heat. Transfer to a clean weighed vessel, cover, and cool promptly under the café's safe procedure. Record cooling method and times; the source does not specify them.
8. Measure total yield by mass and volume. Once a representative sample is at the instrument's required measurement temperature, measure Brix with the calibrated Proster refractometer and record calibration, temperature, and reading. Measure pH only if a calibrated pH meter is available; otherwise record `NOT MEASURED`.
9. Reserve enough product for three cling replicates and two application builds. Load only the service-test portion into the clean house squeeze bottle; record bottle and nozzle opening.
10. Execute GC-20260830-PB001-01. If its advancement rule is met, execute AT-20260830-001.
11. Use the batch for the same-day development session. Refrigerate only while tests are in progress and discard remaining food after the session unless a separately documented observation plan is opened. This is conservative development handling, not a validated shelf-life statement.

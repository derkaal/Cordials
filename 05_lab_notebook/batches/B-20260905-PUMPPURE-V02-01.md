## Planned method

1. Photograph and record the KoRo can's front, ingredient declaration, nutrition, origin, lot, best-before date, net mass, and any opened-storage direction.
2. Clean and sanitise the saucepan, whisk, spatula, thermometer, receiving vessel, cover, and squeeze bottle using the café procedure. Verify the 0.1 g scale and thermometer.
3. Open the can, observe separation, and stir the entire contents until uniform. Record appearance, aroma, texture, and a representative apparent-Brix reading at the recorded sample temperature.
4. Weigh 280 g pumpkin purée and 30 g sugar into the saucepan. In a separate dry cup, mix the other 30 g sugar thoroughly with 2 g NH pectin.
5. Heat the purée and first sugar portion with gentle continuous stirring. At 50°C, gradually add the sugar–pectin premix while whisking.
6. Continue cooking and stirring until the preparation reaches 103°C. Record elapsed times, temperatures, and any scorching, foaming, clumping, or setting.
7. Transfer immediately to a clean weighed shallow vessel, cover with film as the source directs, and cool. Record transfer mass and actual cooling conditions; the source gives no cooling duration or storage claim.
8. Once cold, mix with a spatula until smooth as the source directs. Do not add water or blend unless a later sourced adaptation is opened.
9. Record final yield by mass and volume, apparent Brix and sample temperature, appearance, aroma, taste, and texture. Measure pH only if a calibrated meter is available; otherwise record `NOT MEASURED`.
10. Load only the test portion into the recorded squeeze bottle and run `GC-20260830-PB001-01` against unmodified KoRo purée and FONTE commercial sauce.
11. No shelf life is established. Handle and dispose of the batch under the recorded café development-session procedure.

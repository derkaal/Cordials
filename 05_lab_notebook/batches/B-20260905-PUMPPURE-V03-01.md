## Planned method

1. Photograph and record the KoRo can's front, ingredient declaration, nutrition, origin, lot, best-before date, net mass, and any opened-storage direction.
2. Clean and sanitise the saucepan, whisk, spatula, thermometer, receiving vessel, cover, standard measuring spoon, and squeeze bottle using the café procedure. Verify the thermometer.
3. Open the can, observe separation, and stir the entire contents until uniform. Record appearance, aroma, texture, and a representative apparent-Brix reading at the recorded sample temperature.
4. Weigh 280 g pumpkin purée and 30 g sugar into the saucepan.
5. Level one standard 1/2 tsp of NH pectin without packing it. Mix it thoroughly into the other 30 g sugar in a dry cup. If a scale is available, record the pectin mass as an observation without changing the spoon dose.
6. Heat the purée and first sugar portion with gentle continuous stirring. At 50°C, gradually add the sugar–pectin premix while whisking.
7. Continue cooking and stirring until the preparation reaches 103°C. Record elapsed times, temperatures, and any scorching, foaming, clumping, or setting.
8. Transfer immediately to a clean weighed shallow vessel, cover with film as the source directs, and cool. Record transfer mass and actual cooling conditions; the source gives no cooling duration or storage claim.
9. Once cold, mix with a spatula until smooth as the source directs. Do not add water or blend unless a later sourced adaptation is opened.
10. Record final yield by mass and volume, apparent Brix and sample temperature, appearance, aroma, taste, and texture. Measure pH only if a calibrated meter is available; otherwise record `NOT MEASURED`.
11. Load only the test portion into the recorded squeeze bottle and run `GC-20260830-PB001-01` against unmodified KoRo purée and FONTE commercial sauce.
12. No shelf life is established. Handle and dispose of the batch under the recorded café development-session procedure.

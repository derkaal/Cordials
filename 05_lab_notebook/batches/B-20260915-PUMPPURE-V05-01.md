## Phase A — source-adapted steamed purée

1. Wash the squash. Record whole mass.
2. Peel, halve and remove the stem, seeds, stringy pulp and damaged areas. Record prepared edible mass.
3. Cut into evenly sized cubes.
4. Put the cubes in a steamer or colander over simmering water. Cover and steam for 10 minutes.
5. Test the thickest cubes with the point of a knife. If not cooked through, steam 5 minutes more and test again. Tenderness—not a predicted total time—is the endpoint.
6. Let surface steam escape briefly, then blend completely smooth without adding water, butter or seasoning. Do not sieve by default. Sieve only a small test portion if fibres obstruct the bottle nozzle or spoil the line, and record that action.
7. Record finished mass, texture, apparent Brix and sample temperature. Weigh 280 g for Phase B. If less is available, stop rather than silently rescaling.

## Phase B — unchanged café-practical cling formula

1. Combine 280 g finished purée with the first 30 g sugar.
2. Dry-mix the other 30 g sugar with 1/2 level tsp NH pectin.
3. Heat the purée; at 50°C gradually whisk in the premix.
4. Cook with stirring to 103°C, transfer, cover and cool, then mix smooth with a spatula.
5. Record yield, apparent Brix/sample temperature and compare against KoRo PUREE-V03 under the same service conditions. No shelf life is established.

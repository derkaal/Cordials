## Formulation rationale and provenance

The formula is not copied from a source. PR-0007 supports lychee purée with salt and citric acid but includes alcohol; PR-0016 supports lychee with matcha and milk but uses fresh juice. V01 creates a high-fruit dairy sauce for a different glass-cling function. The paired PB-002/PB-003 design keeps the same 240:60:20:0.5:0.5 base so the first pilot can reveal fruit-driven differences. Approximately 75% of planned ingredient mass is the selected purée.

## Planned method

1. Chill purée and condensed milk to 4 ±1°C. Photograph and record every physical label, lot, best-before date, ingredient declaration, and allergen statement.
2. Verify the scale reads to 0.1 g. Clean and sanitise a narrow blending jug, immersion blender, spatula, receiving vessel, and squeeze bottle using the café procedure; record the procedure.
3. Tare the jug. Weigh 240 g lychee purée, 20 g sugar, 0.5 g citric acid, and 0.5 g salt.
4. Blend for 15 seconds, scrape the jug, then blend for 30 seconds. Do not heat.
5. Weigh 60 g condensed milk separately, add it, and blend on low for 30 seconds. Stop if curdling or grain formation is visible; record rather than correcting the batch.
6. Transfer to a clean weighed vessel, cover, and refrigerate for 15 minutes. Check for undissolved sugar, foam, graininess, separation, colour, aroma, and flow.
7. Record measured yield. Measure Brix only with the calibrated Proster unit at the instrument's required sample temperature; record calibration and temperature. Measure pH only if a calibrated meter is available.
8. Load the documented service-test portion into the measured house squeeze bottle. Run `GC-20260831-PB002-01`; run `AT-20260831-002` only if the candidate advances.
9. Use for the same-day development session and discard remaining food after the session unless a separately documented observation plan is opened. This is not a shelf-life claim.

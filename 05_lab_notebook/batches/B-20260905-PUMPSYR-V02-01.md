## Planned method

1. Photograph and record the Ostmann packet front/back, ingredient and allergen declarations, nutrition, lot, best-before date, net mass, and any manufacturer preparation direction. Record purchase price if available.
2. Use the physical-input record for the KoRo can. If a complete 120 ml portion is not available, stop; do not scale or rebalance this batch during execution.
3. Clean and sanitise the stainless-steel saucepan, whisk, measuring jug/spoons, receiving vessel, and bottle using the café procedure; record it.
4. Measure 300 ml water and 120 ml well-mixed KoRo purée. Weigh 110 g sugar. Measure two level standard teaspoons of Ostmann mix into a tared cup and record their combined mass. Measure 1/2 tsp vanilla.
5. Combine all ingredients in the stainless-steel saucepan over medium-high heat and whisk thoroughly, retaining the PR-0028 method.
6. Stir frequently, heat to a light simmer, and cook until the syrup begins to thicken, approximately 5 minutes. Record actual time and observed temperature; do not invent a tighter endpoint.
7. Remove from heat and transfer to a clean weighed vessel. Record actual cooling conditions because the source does not state them.
8. Record final yield by mass and volume, Brix and sample temperature, appearance, aroma, taste, spice balance, detectable cayenne heat, pourability, and settling. Measure pH only if a calibrated meter is available.
9. Do not assign a matcha dose or shelf life from this batch. Retain it only under the recorded development-session handling/disposition procedure pending a sourced application test.

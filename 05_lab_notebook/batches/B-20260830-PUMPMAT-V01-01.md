## Planned method

1. Photograph and record the physical labels, net contents, best-before dates, and lots for pumpkin purée, condensed milk, and spice mix.
2. Clean and sanitise utensils, saucepan, receiving vessel, and house squeeze bottle using the café's established procedure; record the procedure actually used.
3. Measure every ingredient in the planned source-derived unit. Weigh each volumetric ingredient immediately after measuring and record the observed mass.
4. Combine 31.25 ml water and 31.25 ml sugar in the smallest suitable saucepan. Heat while stirring until the source condition “bring to a boil” is reached; record elapsed time and observed temperature without inventing a target.
5. Add condensed milk, pumpkin purée, spice, and salt. Whisk until visibly uniform and cook for exactly 1 minute, matching PR-0014; record temperature at start/end and any scorching, lumps, or separation.
6. Remove from heat. Transfer to a clean weighed vessel, cover, and cool promptly under the café's safe procedure. Record cooling method and times; the source does not specify them.
7. Measure total yield by mass and volume. Once a representative sample is at the instrument's required measurement temperature, measure Brix with the calibrated Proster refractometer and record calibration, temperature, and reading. Measure pH only if a calibrated pH meter is available; otherwise record `NOT MEASURED`.
8. Reserve enough product for three cling replicates and two application builds. Load only the service-test portion into the clean house squeeze bottle; record bottle and nozzle opening.
9. Execute GC-20260830-PB001-01. If its advancement rule is met, execute AT-20260830-001.
10. Use the batch for the same-day development session. Refrigerate only while tests are in progress and discard remaining food after the session unless a separately documented observation plan is opened. This is conservative development handling, not a validated shelf-life statement.

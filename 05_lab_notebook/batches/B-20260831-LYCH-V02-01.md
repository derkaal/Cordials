## Planned method

1. Complete the canned-input execution gate and prepare at least 222 g smooth drained-fruit purée. Record blender, speed, and time.
2. Verify the scale at 0.1 g and thermometer against the project check. Clean and sanitise the saucepan, whisk, spatula, thermometer, receiving vessel, cover, and squeeze bottle using the café procedure; record it.
3. Weigh 222 g lychee purée and 25 g sugar into the saucepan.
4. In a separate dry cup, mix 25 g sugar thoroughly with 2 g NH pectin.
5. Heat the purée and first sugar portion with continuous gentle stirring. At 50°C, add the sugar–pectin premix gradually while whisking.
6. Continue cooking and stirring until the preparation reaches 103°C. Record start time, 50°C time, 103°C time, and any scorching, foaming, or visible setting.
7. Transfer immediately to a clean weighed vessel, cover with film in the source-stated manner, and cool. Record transfer mass and cooling conditions; do not invent a target cooling time.
8. Once cold, whisk until smooth as the source directs. Record final yield, appearance, aroma, texture, and measured Brix/sample temperature. Measure pH only if a calibrated meter is available.
9. Load the service-test portion into the measured squeeze bottle and run `GC-20260831-PB002-02`. Run `AT-20260831-004` only if the candidate advances.
10. This batch has no validated storage or shelf life. Handle under the café development-session procedure and record actual disposition.

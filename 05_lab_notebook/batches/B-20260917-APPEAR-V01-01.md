## Declared adaptation

PR-0035 uses 500 ml pear juice. This batch uses 250 ml apple juice and 250 ml pear juice. The equal-volume pairing is taken from PR-0036. PR-0035's total juice volume, sugar volume, allspice, cinnamon, citric acid and method are retained.

## Method

1. Record both juice labels, ingredients, nutrition, lot/BBD and whether the pear product is juice or nectar. Measure each juice's apparent Brix and sample temperature separately.
2. Lightly crack whole allspice berries. Do not substitute ground allspice in Batch 001.
3. Combine both juices, measured sugar, allspice, cinnamon stick and citric acid in a saucepan.
4. Heat gently to a simmer while stirring. Stop heating when the sugar has completely dissolved; do not reduce to an invented time or Brix target.
5. Remove from heat and cool under the recorded café procedure.
6. Strain out all spice solids and bottle in a clean measured container.
7. Record yield, apparent Brix and sample temperature, appearance, aroma, apple/pear balance, sweetness, acidity, spice balance and any sediment.
8. Compare at equal measured dose with MONIN Apple Pie for sensory strength and service behaviour. Pear recognition is scored separately because the control is not a pear product.

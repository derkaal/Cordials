## Method

### Phase A — neutral roasted Red Kuri purée

1. Heat a conventional oven to 200°C. Wash and scrub the squash thoroughly; dry it. Record whole mass and photograph/record its condition.
2. Halve it and remove the stem, seeds, stringy pulp and any damaged areas. Keep the clean edible skin. Record prepared edible mass.
3. Cut into large, reasonably even pieces. Place on a parchment-lined tray without oil or seasoning.
4. Roast for 40–50 minutes, checking at 40 minutes. Continue only until every piece is completely fork-tender; record actual time.
5. Blend skin and flesh completely smooth without added liquid. Pass through a fine sieve so skin particles cannot obstruct the squeeze bottle or make a rough glass line.
6. Record finished purée mass, appearance, aroma, texture, and apparent Brix with sample temperature. The Boiron 100% pumpkin reference is 6° ± 2° Brix, but this is a comparison reference—not a pass/fail target for Red Kuri.
7. Weigh 280 g finished purée for Phase B. If less than 280 g is available, stop and record the yield; do not silently rescale this batch.

### Phase B — glass-cling preparation

1. Put 280 g finished roasted purée and 30 g sugar in the saucepan.
2. Dry-mix 30 g sugar with 1 level 1/2 tsp NH pectin.
3. Heat the purée with gentle continuous stirring. At 50°C, gradually whisk in the sugar–pectin premix.
4. Continue stirring and cooking to 103°C. Record time, temperature, scorching, foaming or clumping.
5. Transfer immediately to a clean weighed shallow vessel, cover with film and cool. Once cold, mix with a spatula until smooth.
6. Record yield, apparent Brix and sample temperature, taste, texture and squeeze behaviour. No shelf life is established.

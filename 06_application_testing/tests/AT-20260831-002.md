## Exact preparation and build

1. Use the measured ordinary clear matcha-service glass, clean and dry inside.
2. Whisk 2 g matcha with 40 g water for 20 seconds; record actual water temperature.
3. At time zero apply 40 g sauce as four approximately equal vertical lines on the inner wall. Record actual deposited mass.
4. At 30 seconds add ice over 10 seconds, then milk over 10 seconds, then matcha over 10 seconds. Do not stir.
5. Photograph immediately, at 60 seconds, and at 300 seconds after build completion.
6. Taste once unstirred, then stir exactly three full rotations and reassess lychee identity, matcha clarity, sweetness, acidity, dairy integration, graininess, and overall balance.
7. Prepare two house and two control drinks if measured batch yield permits, alternating sample order.

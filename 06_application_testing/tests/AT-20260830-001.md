## Exact preparation and build

1. Chill the clean, dry glass inverted for at least 30 minutes at 4 ± 1 °C. Condition sauce and milk at 4 ± 1 °C.
2. Sift 2.0 g matcha into the standard whisking vessel. Add 40.0 g water at 75 ± 2 °C and whisk for 20 seconds using the same tool and motion for every build. Prepare this immediately before wall application and record elapsed time to pour.
3. Apply 18.0 g sauce as three separate vertical 6.0 g × approximately 60 mm lines inside the glass, starting 30 mm below the rim. Bring glass upright; this is `t = 0`. Photograph.
4. At `t = 30 seconds`, add 100 ± 5 g ice centrally over 10 seconds, avoiding deliberate scraping of the wall; record unavoidable contact.
5. Pour 150.0 g cold whole milk centrally over 10 seconds.
6. Pour the prepared matcha centrally over 10 seconds. Target completion is approximately `t = 60 seconds`; record actual completion time rather than forcing an invalid run.
7. Photograph immediately after completion, then at 60 seconds and 300 seconds after completion. Record sauce descent, pooling, pattern legibility, colour bleed, separation, and damage from ice.
8. At 60 seconds after build completion, assess aroma and take one unstirred sip from the same position/procedure for every build. Then stir exactly three complete rotations with the same spoon, wait 15 seconds, and assess integrated flavour, sweetness, pumpkin clarity, matcha intensity, texture, and residue.
9. Serve/test samples in alternating order between replicates. If blind coding is practical, use neutral codes and decode after notes are complete.

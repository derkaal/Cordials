# Autumn Nutty Candidate Screen — Pistachio, Roasted Hazelnut, Black Sesame

Date: 2026-09-05

## Decision

Open three research-stage product cases and exclude chestnut:

- `PB-004` Pistachio Paste Preparation and Syrup
- `PB-005` Roasted Hazelnut Paste Preparation and Syrup
- `PB-006` Black Sesame Paste Preparation

No project batch or house formulation is opened by this screen. A commercial product listing proves availability or an application, not a reproducible house formula.

## Terminology

Pistachio and hazelnut inputs are **nut pastes/mus**, not fruit purées. Black sesame is a **seed paste**. These high-fat materials can separate and may require an emulsification strategy; that strategy cannot be invented and must come from a complete professional/commercial formulation or be recorded as a disclosed adaptation.

## Why whole nuts are not the Batch 001 default

- The first development question is flavour and drink function, not whether the café can manufacture nut butter.
- A purchased single-ingredient paste removes roasting, skin, particle-size and extraction-yield variables from the first comparison.
- Whole-nut processing may become worthwhile only after projected volume, labour, cleaning, usable yield and measured cost per serve are known.
- A standard café blender is not assumed to produce a stable fine nut paste. Equipment capability must be demonstrated rather than inferred.
- Nut and sesame allergen handling, storage and cleaning requirements apply whichever input route is chosen.

## Research gates before any batch

1. Select one complete professional formulation for each intended house preparation; do not reverse-engineer MONIN or 1883.
2. Preserve syrup and paste as separate formats. Do not call a paste a purée or claim that it behaves like a cordial.
3. Convert any source volume or spoon units only through a documented measurement or authoritative product-specific conversion; the final operational batch must use practical whole-gram quantities where possible.
4. Decide whether the first application is matcha, coffee or cocktail for each product and select an exact sourced build or disclosed adaptation.
5. Record physical labels, lots, purchase prices, allergen statements and oil separation before testing.
6. Do not open shelf-life optimisation until flavour, texture, dispersion and service utility have passed.

## Geographic research control

The evidence pool now explicitly distinguishes the base of the responsible operator from flavour associations. PB-006 has validated European (`PR-0026`, Germany) and Asian (`PR-0029`–`PR-0031`, Japan) professional/commercial references. PB-004 and PB-005 currently have European application sources but no qualifying Asia-based product-specific formulation. Those gaps remain open in `geographic_recipe_coverage.md`; they are not permission to invent recipes or weaken source completeness.

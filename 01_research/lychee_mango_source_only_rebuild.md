# PB-002 / PB-003 — Source-Only Professional Rebuild

Date: 2026-08-31

## Governing constraint

Active recipe, batch, and application formulations must be exact sourced recipes or explicit adaptations of sourced recipes from commercially active/professional sources. The discarded V01 lychee and mango plans were unexecuted original formulations and are not retained as development candidates.

## Candidate decision

Use a 20% scale of the Boiron source ratios because it creates the smallest useful, directly weighable pilot:

| Fruit | Purée | Sugar | NH pectin | Planned ingredient mass |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Lychee | 200 g | 45 g | 1.8 g | 246.8 g |
| Mango | 200 g | 45 g | 1.4 g | 246.4 g |

The 0.1 g pectin values and 22.5 g half-sugar portions are intentional source-proportional calculations that are directly measurable on the required 0.1 g scale. No volume fractions or sub-0.1 g quantities are used.

For lychee, the first available-input plan replaces Boiron purée with a purée made from drained canned lychees while preserving the Boiron ratio and method. That ingredient substitution makes the batch an `ADAPTATION OF SOURCED RECIPE`. It may not run until the can label, syrup type, drained yield, and fruit Brix are recorded. A future exact-brand Boiron-input batch remains possible through `SRC-2026-062`.

For mango, the smaller scale and service-purpose change make the operational batch an `ADAPTATION OF SOURCED RECIPE`, while the formula ratio and method remain source-proportional.

## Texture boundary

NH pectin is part of PR-0019 and PR-0020. It is therefore not a separate house texture modification. Do not run an unpectinated or altered-pectin aliquot under the same batch ID. Any later change to pectin type or amount must be a new, explicit adaptation of the professional source and must show the source amount beside the changed amount.

## Application boundary

Both fruit drinks will adapt PR-0017: 2 g matcha, 60 ml water at about 80°C, 200 ml cold milk, two tablespoons of fruit component, and ice. The source's two tablespoons will be operationalised as 30 ml, with actual deposited mass recorded. The adaptations are the source-derived sauce, whole milk in place of plant milk, and application to the inner glass wall rather than the bottom. Ice quantity remains source-unstated and is controlled as a measured glass-specific method parameter rather than invented as a source quantity.

## Shelf-life boundary

The 103°C endpoint is a source-stated texture process, not proof of ambient stability or a validated refrigerated life. Flavour, texture, glass cling, squeeze service, and drink performance must pass before a freeze–thaw quality study is opened.

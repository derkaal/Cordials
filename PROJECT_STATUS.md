## Blocked decisions

- PB-001 dairy remains permitted, but neither selected component batch contains dairy. Standard café equipment is sufficient; Russell Hobbs 25630-56 is not part of either source method. Exact glass/nozzle and scale/thermometer checks remain pre-test measurements.
- PB-001 numeric cling threshold is intentionally `TBD` until controls show the useful measurement range.
- Product-specific Brix targets are intentionally `NOT YET SET`. Brix comparison begins immediately when EQ-002 is calibrated, but a final target window can only be derived from measured controls and house batches that pass flavour, texture and service function.
- PB-001 shelf-life target and any safety claim remain `TBD`; no source-stated period transfers to an original or adapted formula.
- PB-002/PB-003 application adaptations use dairy because it is permitted; the purée preparations themselves contain no dairy. No handling period has been validated.
- The canned-lychee batch cannot run until its label, drained yield, and Brix are recorded and at least 220 g smooth drained-fruit purée is confirmed.
- Sous vide is not required for any active Batch 001: PB-002/PB-003 require an open 103°C cook. Vacuum sealing is packaging, not a shelf-life control; freeze-first use requires a later quality test and appropriate food-safety controls.
- PR-0021's mango cordial uses a sealed bag under refrigeration, not a heated sous-vide bath. The CASO VC10's suitability for sealing this unfrozen liquid process is unresolved; do not substitute a freeze-first step into the source method.
- PR-0022's lychee cordial cannot be declared exact until the 560 g can basis and treatment of its canning liquid are resolved against the on-hand product.
- The health review does not authorise a “healthy,” “healthier,” “low sugar,” or similar claim. PUREE-V03 retains sucrose while disclosing its 3 g reduction from the exact 280 g PR-0027 scale; SYRUP-V02 retains the source sucrose and also contains the cane sugar in its measured Ostmann dose. Alternatives remain a later, separately sourced comparison only after flavour, texture, and service function pass.
